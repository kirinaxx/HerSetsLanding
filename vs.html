HerSets vs booking apps

Booking apps are built around the calendar. HerSets is built around the client: the lash map, the nail formula, the color you mixed, the sensitivity you must not forget. Plenty of artists use both.

HerSets vs Square, Vagaro and GlossGenius

These platforms are scheduling and payments first. Client details usually live in a notes field. HerSets flips that: records first, with a booking page and SMS reminders when you want them.

HerSets vs Glamorph

Glamorph is the closest match: it also keeps client details, notes, photos and service history in one place. HerSets goes deeper per discipline, with purpose-built maps and formulas for each craft, and adds Salon View so a whole chair lineup sits on one screen. Questions

Does HerSets replace my booking app?

It can. HerSets has a built-in booking page with SMS appointment reminders. Or use it alongside your existing platform (Square, Vagaro, GlossGenius) as your dedicated client notes hub.

Can I use HerSets and a booking app together?

Yes. Many artists keep their booking platform for the calendar and payments, and use HerSets for the details that do not fit in a notes box: lash maps, nail formulas, hair color formulas, skin notes.

What does HerSets cost compared with booking platforms?

HerSets is free forever for up to 10 clients. After that, Basic is $8.99/month ($79.99/year) and SMS Pro is $17.99/month ($159.99/year). GlossGenius lists plans from $24/month at the time of writing, so check each platform's own pricing page for current numbers.

About HerSets

HerSets is a digital notebook app built specifically for beauty artists — lash technicians, nail artists, hair stylists, and estheticians. It lets you record client preferences, formulas, service maps, photos, and full appointment history in one organized place. No more scrolling through camera rolls for a lash map, or searching texts for what formula you used last time.

HerSets is built for independent beauty professionals: lash artists, nail techs, hair stylists, hair extension specialists, and estheticians. Whether you work from home, rent a salon suite, or travel to clients — HerSets is built for the way you actually work, not for a front desk.

General notes apps give you a blank page. HerSets gives you structured templates built for beauty services — lash maps with zones, nail formulas per finger, hair color placement records, skin analysis maps. Every client has a dedicated profile that updates automatically after each visit. You get instant search, allergy alerts, and service history — not a wall of text with no structure.

It can — HerSets includes a built-in booking page where you can track your appointments and send SMS reminders to clients. If you already use a booking platform like Square, Vagaro, GlossGenius, or Acuity, HerSets works great alongside it as your client notes and formula hub. But if you'd prefer to keep everything in one place, the booking feature is there whenever you're ready to use it.

Features & Templates

You can track:

  • Client profiles — name, contact, allergies, skin type, eye shape, personal notes
  • Lash maps — curl, style, diameter, and adhesive per eye zone
  • Nail formulas — gel system, color, shape, and add-ons per finger
  • Hair color records — placement maps with zone-by-zone formula
  • Skin session notes — face analysis, treatments, product reactions
  • Photos — before/after images tied to each service record
  • Full service history — every appointment on a client's timeline
  • Custom fields — anything specific to your specialty

HerSets ships with five ready-to-use templates:

  • Lash Map — five eye zones with curl, style, diameter, and adhesive notes
  • Nail Design Builder — per-finger color formulas, gel system, shape, and add-ons
  • Hair Color Record — placement diagram with sectioned formula fields
  • Skin Session — face analysis map with treatment and product notes
  • Hair Extensions — method, color match, length, and maintenance notes
